Field Intelligence — 5 directions

The same real bracket data (The Village Mini Tournament — 8 divisions, live DUPR ratings, real ringers) rendered five genuinely different ways. Each makes a different bet about how a competitive player evaluates a field before registering. Switch with the tabs, or hit All for the contact sheet.

Mobile frames · 390px · the primary surface

Each frame is the live mockup, isolated in its own iframe.

Tale of the Tape — The field as a fight-card weigh-in — contenders ranked top to bottom, ringers flagged.
The Spectrum — One continuous rating axis. Window lit, every player a dot, ringers glowing past the cap.
The Terminal — All brackets at once, dense and sortable. Triage the whole tournament without drilling in.
The Scout's Report — A beat writer's verdict in warm prose, then the stat block. Reads, doesn't operate.
The Tier Ladder — Players grouped into rating tiers. Which shelf am I on, and who's above me?

Contact sheet — all five at a glance.
